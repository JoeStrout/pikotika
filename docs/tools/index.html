Tools

Everything here runs in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Converter

Try

You do not have to say which notation you are writing in — the converter works it out and writes all four back. That covers pasting Han and getting Latin, and pasting Latin and getting Han, in the same box. Tap any word of the Latin line to open its entry and hear it.

English goes in one word or phrase at a time: it looks up a root, a standing compound, or a name, not a sentence. Pikotika (in Latin, gloss, or Han form) can take a full sentence.

More tools

Three of these live on the topic pages that explain what they do, which is where they are most use.

  • Name adapter — type your own name and watch the two passes run, one substitution at a time.
  • Number reader — any number, however long, said out loud: decimals, fractions, percentages and ordinals too.
  • Clock reader — set a time and hear it both ways, on a twelve-hour clock and a twenty-four-hour one.
  • Vocab search — every root, compound, name and loanword, searchable and filterable.
