Seasons

A season is an anyoparte year-part. Each one is named for what the time of year brings, so there are no new roots to learn — only four familiar ones with tempo on the end.

The four seasons

  • arpotempo spring plant-time 木时
  • wotempo summer hot-time 火时
  • pomotempo autumn, fall fruit-time 果时
  • rikustempo winter cold-time 冷时

Wet and dry

Much of the world has two seasons rather than four, and they are named the same way.

  • ruvatempo rainy season rain-time 雨时
  • nonruvatempo dry season not-rain-time 不雨时

Saying when

A season behaves like any other time word. Use in for in, tis for this, and yer or tar after the amount to count backward or forward — exactly as with days.

PikotikaLiterallyMeaning
in wotempo in hot-timein summer
tis wotempo this hot-timethis summer
2 wotempo yer 2 hot-time beforetwo summers ago

Examples

Where are you going for summer vacation?

In wotempo yuveniire, tu ri ire ver kerroko?

Unfortunately, the food supplies gave out before the end of winter.

Marumpos, rikustempovin yer, tenekomi ri vin.

The months and weekdays that divide the year up more finely get their own page, under Dates and weekdays.
