Weather

Weather is ventomoto air-manner. Most of what you say about it has no subject at all: English says it's raining, and Pikotika just says Ri ruva.

Weather roots

PikotikaMeaning
ruvarain; to rain
ventoair, wind, sky
yansun, sunlight; day
wohot, heat; fire
rikuscold, cool; ice
akuwater; wet

It's raining

Weather takes an indefinite subject: open with ri and say what's happening.

PikotikaMeaning
Ri ruva.It's raining.
Ri rikusruva.It's snowing.
Ri mekavento.It's very windy.
Ri ventovorto.There's thunder and lightning.
Ri yanvento.It's sunny.
Ri ruvavento.It's cloudy.
Ri meka wo.It's very hot.
Ri surmesur rikus.It's too cold.
Ri non ruva.It isn't raining.

The aspect markers follow it in the usual way.

PikotikaMeaning
Ri ruva apa.It's starting to rain.
Ri ruva sista.It's still raining.
Ruva ri vin.The rain has stopped.

Talking about the weather

PikotikaMeaning
Ventomoto ri kermoto?How's the weather?
Tis ri pona yan, non?Nice day, isn't it?
Tisyan, ventomoto ri meka pona.The weather is very good today.
Ventomoto ri veros artiven vin.The weather changed fast.
In suryan, ri akuvento.It's foggy in the morning.
Eko ri rikus.I'm cold.
Eko ri wo.I'm hot.

Temperature

Temperature is womesur hot-amount. Pikotika is used around the world, so a temperature is in Celsius unless you say otherwise; to give it in Fahrenheit, add Veranaito.

PikotikaMeaning
Womesur ri ker?What's the temperature?
Womesur ri 30.It's 30 degrees (Celsius).
Tisyan, womesur ri 86 Veranaito.Today it's 86 degrees Fahrenheit.

Forecasts and plans

PikotikaMeaning
Yantar, ri pos ruva.It might rain tomorrow.
Ri 30 inkaton ruva pos.There's a 30% chance of rain.
Pam toma a ruvauter.Take an umbrella.
Pos ri ruva, tisrason ekomen ri sista in kase.If it rains, we'll stay home.

Climate

For what a place is usually like, add a season or a place.

PikotikaMeaning
Ri nonmurtempo ruva in tisroko.It rarely rains here.
In wotempo, ri meka wo.It's very hot in summer.
In rikustempo, ri mur rikusruva in tisroko.It snows a lot here in winter.
In tisroko, ri ruvatempo.It's the rainy season here.

A whole conversation

Bob wants to go for a walk.

PikotikaEnglish
Popu: Tisyan, ekomen ri kampaire ver verteroko, ker?Bob: Shall we walk to the park today?
Aras: Ri ruvavento. Ri pos ruva.Alice: It's cloudy. It might rain.
Popu: Son 30 inkaton.Bob: Only thirty percent.
Aras: Pona. Sets eko ri toma a ruvauter.Alice: Fine. But I'm taking an umbrella.
Popu: Ri ruva apa!Bob: It's starting to rain!
Aras: Eko ri tika a tis vin!Alice: I told you so!
Popu: Eko ri kan sista tun tu ruvauter, ker?Bob: Can I stand under your umbrella?
Aras: Si. Kum tar, ekomen ri pos vite a kororkurva!Alice: Yes. And afterward, we might see a rainbow!
