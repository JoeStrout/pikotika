Negation

One word negates anything: put non in front of it. A second word, nem, says that there isn't any.

non — not

It goes immediately before whatever it denies, and denies exactly that.

PikotikaMeaning
Eko ri non kansa.I'm not tired.
Eko ri non komi a akupeste.I don't eat fish.
Tis ri non tuke.They're not sweet.
Non rikus, pam.Not cold, please.

It is also the word for ‘no’ in an answer, and the tag that turns a statement into a leading question — see Questions and answers.

nem — none, there isn't

nem is not a stronger non; it is a different claim. non denies a description, nem denies that the thing is there at all.

PikotikaMeaning
Non rikus, pam.Not cold, please. (bring it warm)
Nem rikus, pam.No ice, please. (leave it out)
Ri nem marumkosa.There's no problem.
Eko ri vake a nem.I did nothing.

Not happy is not the same as sad

Written as two words, non konten says only ‘not happy’ — someone who simply isn't cheerful. Close it up into one word and non takes its other reading, un-, and you get a positive claim about the opposite state.

PikotikaMeaning
non kontennot happy
nonkontensad
non masnot more
nonmasless

One more member of the family: nonves (not-true) is the plain adjective ‘false’, and it is also what opens a condition you know to be false — see Conditions and counterfactuals.

Try it yourself

  1. Tis ri non ves.
    Show answer

    ‘That isn't true.’ Compare Tis ri nonves., ‘That's false’ — the same claim, stated positively.

  2. Say ‘There's no hot water.’ (Hot water is wo aku.)
    Show answer

    Ri nem wo aku. — none present, so nem.

  3. Tis ri non wosapor.
    Show answer

    ‘It's not spicy.’
