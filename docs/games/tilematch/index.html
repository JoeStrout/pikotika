Tile Match

Mahjong solitaire over the Pikotika roots. Every root is two tiles — one with its two English meanings, one with its written form in Latin and Han. Clear the board by matching them.

A tile can only be taken when nothing sits on top of it and one of its long sides is open, exactly as in the paper game. Pick a level and the board is dealt from its roots; no level has quite the forty-eight it takes to fill the board, so the rest come from the level before — a little revision with every game.
