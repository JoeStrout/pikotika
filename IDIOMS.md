# Pikotika Idioms and Stock Phrases

Multi-word expressions whose meaning is conventional rather than merely the sum of
their parts -- greetings, courtesies, and the set phrases that come up constantly in
travel and small talk.

Single-word compounds live in [COMPOUNDS.md](COMPOUNDS.md); the roots themselves are
in [ROOTS.md](ROOTS.md).  **This table is maintained by hand** -- unlike the other
two, it is not generated from a TSV, so edit it directly.

The English column gives the closest English idiom, not a literal rendering; the
gloss column is where the literal reading lives.  A translation of this page into
another language should substitute that language's own idiom, or a plain explanation
where it has none.

## Figures of speech

These are metaphors that many unrelated languages arrived at independently, which is
why they are worth having: a learner usually recognizes the picture before learning
the phrase.

| English | Gloss | Latin | Han |
|---|---|---|---|
| Two birds with one stone. | two bird, one stone | **Pits avis, wun rapis.** | 2 鳥, 1 岩. |
| It's a small world. | earth RI small | **Tera ri piko.** | 土 ⊢ 小. |
| Time is money. | time RI money | **Tempo ri moni.** | 时 ⊢ 贝. |
| The apple doesn't fall far from the tree. | plant-child RI remain near plant | **Arponino ri sista nir arpo.** | 木子 ⊢ 止 近 木. |

## Set phrases

Common fixed expressions people just memorize and use in the appropriate situation.

| English | Gloss | Latin | Han |
|---|---|---|---|
| I'm happy to meet you. | self RI happy meet A you | **Eko ri konten sarve a tuo.** | 己 ⊢ 楽 会 ⇒ 尔. |
| No problem.  You're welcome. | no bad-thing | **Nem marumkosa.** | 无 悪物. |
| Never mind.  It doesn't matter. | no thing | **Nem kosa.** | 无 物. |
| Enjoy your meal.  (*bon appétit*) | good food | **Pona komi.** | 好 食. |
| Take care.  Safe travels. | good go | **Pona ire.** | 好 行. |
| After you. | you before | **Tuo yer.** | 尔 前. |
| I'm just looking. | self RI only see | **Eko ri son vite.** | 己 ⊢ 只 見. |
| It's on me.  I'll pay. | self RI buy | **Eko ri kompar.** | 己 ⊢ 買. |
| I see (understand.) | self RI see | **Eko ri vite.** | 己 ⊢ 見. |
| There's a 30% chance of rain. | RI 30 in-hundred rain if | **Ri 30 inkaton ruva pos.** | ⊢ 30 内百 雨 可. |
| little by little, gradually | small after small | **piko tar piko** | 小 后 小 |
| one by one | one after one | **wun tar wun** | 1 后 1 |
| day after day | sun after sun | **sore tar sore** | 日 后 日 |
| They say that ... | RI say A ... | **RI tika a ...** |  ⊢ 云 ⇒ ... |
