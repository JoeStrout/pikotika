# Pikotika Idioms and Stock Phrases

Multi-word expressions whose meaning is conventional rather than merely the sum of
their parts -- proverbs, courtesies, and the set phrases that come up constantly in
travel and small talk.

Single-word compounds live in [COMPOUNDS.md](COMPOUNDS.md); the roots themselves are
in [ROOTS.md](ROOTS.md).  **This table is maintained by hand** -- unlike the other
two, it is not generated from a TSV, so edit it directly.

The English column gives the closest English idiom, not a literal rendering; the
gloss column is where the literal reading lives.  A translation of this page into
another language should substitute that language's own idiom, or a plain explanation
where it has none.

## What belongs here

**Pikotika stays literal by default, and this page is meant to stay short.**  An idiom
is by definition something you cannot work out from its parts, so every one of them is
a hidden vocabulary item -- a penalty for exactly the learner the language exists to
serve, and an advantage to whoever grew up with the picture it came from.

A figure of speech is admitted only if unrelated languages have already arrived at the
same picture, so that a learner recognizes it before being taught it.  'Two birds with
one stone' passes: English, German, Chinese 一石二鳥 and Japanese all say it.  'Piece of
cake' fails, and fails instructively -- Spanish says *pan comido* (eaten bread),
Japanese 朝飯前 (before breakfast), Russian "simpler than a steamed turnip."  There is
no shared picture to recognize, so a Pikotika coinage would be a 201st root in
disguise.  Say **nonsorin**, 'not hard'.

**A figure that earns its keep usually becomes a root sense or a compound instead, and
that is the better home for it.**  The language is already full of them: **pesaneses**
(*heavy-necessary*) 'important', **vite** covering 'understand' as well as 'see',
**rikusomo** (*cold-person*) 'unfriendly', **mekaruva** (*big-rain*) 'storm',
**testamara** (*head-sick*) 'headache, nuisance'.  Those are productive rather than
fixed, and they are indexed where a learner will look.  This page is for the residue.

## Proverbs and sayings

Sayings quotable as a piece of wisdom.  These are safe at any number: each is a
literally true sentence on its face, so a listener who misses the point still parses
the words.

| English | Gloss | Latin | Han |
|---|---|---|---|
| Two birds with one stone. | two bird, one stone | **Pits avis, wun rapis.** | 2 鳥, 1 岩. |
| It's a small world. | earth RI small | **Tera ri piko.** | 土 ⊢ 小. |
| Time is money. | time RI money | **Tempo ri moni.** | 时 ⊢ 贝. |
| The apple doesn't fall far from the tree. | fruit RI remain near plant | **Pomo ri sista nir arpo.** | 果 ⊢ 止 近 木. |
| Beauty is in the eye of the beholder. | good-see RI in see-person think | **Ponavite ri in viteomo pensa.** | 好見 ⊢ 内 見人 思. |

## Set phrases

Fixed expressions people memorize and use in the appropriate situation.  These are the
real idioms -- the ones whose meaning does not fall out of the words -- so keep the
list short.

| English | Gloss | Latin | Han |
|---|---|---|---|
| I'm happy to meet you. | I RI happy meet A you | **Eko ri konten sarve a tu.** | 己 ⊢ 楽 会 ⇒ 尔. |
| No problem.  You're welcome. | no bad-thing | **Nem marumkosa.** | 无 悪物. |
| Never mind.  It doesn't matter. | no thing | **Nem kosa.** | 无 物. |
| Enjoy your meal.  (*bon appétit*) | good food | **Pona komi.** | 好 食. |
| Take care.  Safe travels. | good go | **Pona ire.** | 好 行. |
| Have fun.  Enjoy yourself. | good play | **Pona yu.** | 好 玩. |
| Good night. | good no-sun | **Pona nemyan.** | 好 无日. |
| Get well.  Get well soon. | good heal | **Pona sana.** | 好 医. |
| After you. | you before | **Tu yer.** | 尔 前. |
| I'm just looking. | I RI only see | **Eko ri son vite.** | 己 ⊢ 只 見. |
| It's on me.  I'll pay. | I RI buy | **Eko ri kompar.** | 己 ⊢ 買. |
| I see (understand.) | I RI see | **Eko ri vite.** | 己 ⊢ 見. |

## Patterns

Not idioms but frames: each takes an argument, and stays productive wherever you use
it.  See [Prepositional phrases](https://pikotika.org/grammar/prepositions/) for
**in ... moto** in its grammatical setting.

| English | Gloss | Latin | Han |
|---|---|---|---|
| by means of; by way of; via | in ... manner | **in ... moto** | 内 ... 方 |
| They say that ... | RI say A ... | **Ri tika a ...** | ⊢ 言 ⇒ ... |
| little by little, gradually | small after small | **piko tar piko** | 小 后 小 |
| one by one | one after one | **wun tar wun** | 1 后 1 |
| day after day | sun after sun | **yan tar yan** | 日 后 日 |
