Travel and directions

Most travel terms come from two roots: ire is go, move, travel, and veni is come, arrive. A trip is veniire come-go — the going and the coming back, in one word.

Where are you going?

ver to marks the destination and vons from marks where you started. Ask with kerroko what-place, sitting in the slot the answer will fill.

PikotikaMeaning

Toreta ri kerroko?Where is the bathroom?
Eko ri ire ver sitas Rispan.I'm going to Lisbon.
Eko ri veni vons eksenasyon.I've come from abroad.
Tu ri viva in tis sitas, ker?Do you live here in the city?

Walking and driving have their own verbs, so Eko ri kampaire is the natural way to say ‘I'm going on foot’ — no moto phrase needed.

A ticket is just karta card — the same root as a page, a card, or any other printed slip.

Giving directions

Four words do nearly all the work: sini left, rem right, reto straight, and kurva turn. Directions are commands, so they start with the verb — with pam in front to be polite.

PikotikaMeaning
Pam reto ire.Go straight.
Pam kurva ver sini.Turn left.
Pam kurva ver rem in tarorten kurvaroko.Turn right at the next corner.
Pam reto ire ver nontis meka arpo, tar pam kurva ver sini.Go straight to that big tree, then turn left.
Sini in arpo.Left at the tree.
Pam 5 parte turan kampaire.Walk five minutes.
Kamar 12, sur in surmoto, in rem.Room twelve, up the stairs, on the right.
Tu ri vite a tis.You'll see it.

Near, far, and how long

PikotikaMeaning
Karoroko ri nir, non?The station is near, isn't it?
Tis ri ronka moto.That's a long way.
Tis ri meka ron.It's very far.
Nir.Almost. (nearly there)
In 20 kirumitar.About twenty kilometers.
Eko ri viva in pits ora turan ver sur.I live about two hours north.
Si, sets kampaire ri mas veros.Yes, but walking is faster.

turan interval marks a span, and it is a time word — it belongs after a unit that could otherwise be read as a point or a portion. ora is both ‘hour’ and ‘o’clock’, and parte is both ‘part’ and ‘minute’, so 5 parte turan is five minutes. A unit of measure has no such rival reading, so a distance takes the bare number: 20 kirumitar. The metric units — mitar, ritar, kuram — are sanctioned loanwords.
