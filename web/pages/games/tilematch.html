Tile Match

Mahjong solitaire over the Pikotika roots. Every root is two tiles — one with its two English meanings, one with its written form in Latin and Han. Clear the board by matching them.

A tile can only be taken when nothing sits on top of it and one of its long sides is open, exactly as in the paper game. Forty-one Level 1 roots make forty-eight pairs, so a few roots turn up twice.
