A tiny language for real conversation

Pikotika is a constructed international language of about 200 roots, built for travel and small talk. Its name is piko-tika — small talk. It is designed to be learnable in a weekend and forgiving of accents.

Where is the bathroom?

Panyu ri kerroko?

Please wait one moment.

Pam sista wun pikotempo.
