Pikotika Han
------------

pikotika-han-regular.woff2, pikotika-han-bold.woff2

A subset of Noto Sans CJK JP, containing only the ~200 characters Pikotika
uses, with one glyph added: U+22A2 TURNSTILE (the particle RI), which the
source face does not have.  That glyph is drawn from the source face's own
stroke weights and math axis -- see gen_han_font.py in the repository root,
which builds these files.
